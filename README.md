# David Battistelli — Engineering Portfolio

My personal portfolio, bringing together selected research and engineering work in numerical modelling, fusion energy, thermal analysis and product development.

The site presents my background, international experience and approach to engineering problems. Each project explains its purpose, my contribution and the resulting work, with supporting theses, presentations or technical reports.

**[Visit the portfolio](https://davidbattistelli.github.io/my_portfolio/)**

## Selected projects

- **TRUST–MEQ Modelling Framework** — Master's thesis developed in collaboration with the Swiss Plasma Center–EPFL, adapting a numerical framework for tokamak equilibrium modelling and plasma reconstruction to TRUST.
- **TRUST Tokamak — Stabilising Plates** — Bachelor's thesis examining the relationship between stabilising plate positions, plasma stability and electromagnetic loads.
- **PVT Panel Thermal Analysis** — Thermal modelling of photovoltaic–thermal panels during my internship at MG Sustainable Engineering AB in Sweden.
- **Product Development: From Concept to Virtual Prototype** — Development of a product from scratch, covering concept and CAD design, material selection, thermal and mechanical analyses, and preliminary life cycle assessment.

The portfolio also includes my CV and links to the documents behind these projects.
